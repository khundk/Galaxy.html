The YSA Story

A Century of
American Elegance

In 1924, on a quiet street in Newport, Rhode Island, YSA began as a small workshop crafting polo shirts for the gentlemen of the horse country. Word spread quickly — our shirts fit better, lasted longer, and looked sharper than anything else on the field.

A hundred years later, little has changed. We still source the finest cotton from the American South, still cut every pattern by hand, and still believe that the best clothes are the ones you reach for without thinking. Each YSA garment carries the quiet confidence of a life well lived.

Today, YSA is worn on polo fields, in boardrooms, and at Sunday dinner tables across the country. It is not fashion. It is a way of dressing — and a way of living.

— YSA, Est. 1924

The Art of the Perfect Polo

Dressing for the Season

Spring demands layers, not loudness. A navy blazer over a cream oxford. Khaki trousers with a woven belt. Let the clothes speak softly — as a gentleman should.

Caring for Fine Cloth

Wash cold, hang dry, press with steam. A well-made garment rewards patience. Treat your YSA pieces with respect and they will serve you for decades.
